SUGGESTED EXAMINER WALKTHROUGH (approx. 10 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Open https://pulsetriage.vercel.app and sign in as patient@ug.edu.gh
    / password123.

 2. Click "Start Symptom Triage". Select "Chest Pain / Palpitations",
    duration "Sudden (< 6 hours)", pain level 9, and tick the red flag
    "Chest pain or pressure radiating to arm/jaw". Evaluate.
    EXPECTED: EMERGENCY at score 95, emergency-services redirect displayed,
    and NO booking option offered anywhere on the result view.
    This is requirement FR-2.6, the system's safety-critical behaviour.

 3. Run a second triage: "Severe Headache / Dizziness / Numbness",
    duration "Sudden (< 6 hours)", pain 7, no red flags.
    EXPECTED: URGENT at score 71, Neurology recommended, book within 24 hours.

 4. Click "Book Slot with Recommended Specialist". Choose a doctor, date and
    30-minute slot. At payment, choose Mobile Money and enter 0241234567.
    EXPECTED: reference PAY-SIM-nnnnnn issued; appointment becomes CONFIRMED.
    To see the declined path, enter 00000 instead - the appointment stays in
    PENDING_PAYMENT and can be retried.

 5. Sign out and sign in as dr.mensah@ug.edu.gh / password123.
    EXPECTED: the patient queue is ordered by clinical urgency (highest
    severity first), NOT by booking time.

 6. Sign out and sign in as admin@ug.edu.gh / password123.
    Visit /admin (metrics and doctor verification), /admin/rules (triage rule
    list and simulator) and /admin/audit (append-only audit trail).

DISCLOSED LIMITATIONS (full detail in Technical_Debt_Plan)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following are deliberate, documented limitations of this 48-hour
demonstrator, not undiscovered faults:

  * The API layer does not yet enforce server-side authorisation (TD-01).
    Please do not enter real personal or medical data into the deployment.
  * Sessions are held unsigned in browser storage (TD-03).
  * Payments are fully simulated - no money moves (TD-11).
  * Notifications are in-application only - no e-mail or SMS is sent (TD-12).
  * The consultation room shows a local camera preview only; it does not
    yet carry two-way media between participants (TD-10).
  * Administrator triage-rule edits are session-scoped and do not affect
    the patient-facing engine (TD-04).
  * Credentials committed early in the project remain in Git history pending
    rotation and a history purge (TD-02). The working tree is clean and is
    verified by "npm run scan:secrets".
  * The clinical rule content is illustrative and has NOT been validated by
    a qualified clinical advisor.

Defects closed since first issue:

  * D-03 (clinical) - "High fever with neck stiffness" and "Uncontrolled or
    heavy bleeding" were shown to patients as red flags but were not bound to
    any EMERGENCY rule, so they added 10 points instead of triggering the
    safety short-circuit. RULE-007 and RULE-008 now bind them. Verify with:
        npm run test:unit        (TC-UNIT-03, TC-UNIT-13, TC-UNIT-14)
    Or in the live app: run a triage with pain 2/10 and tick "Uncontrolled or
    heavy bleeding" - the result must be EMERGENCY with no booking offered.

  * D-02 (security, code half) - hard-coded credentials removed from source,
    .env untracked, .gitignore corrected, secret scanner added. Verify with:
        npm run scan:secrets
